==============================================================================
  CG LEARNING ROADMAP — Oct–Dec 2026
  Reference only. Lessons written on demand. Order matters — do not skip ahead.
==============================================================================

  Everything below flows from one core idea:
    How do you DESCRIBE a surface → how does LIGHT interact with it →
    how do you COMPUTE that interaction EFFICIENTLY at scale →
    how do you compute it CORRECTLY (ground truth)?

  Textures, PBR, shadow maps, deferred rendering, and ray tracing are not
  separate topics. They are the same question answered at different levels.

  THEME B — LIGHT–SURFACE INTERACTION (THE RENDERING EQUATION)
  "How does light physically interact with this surface?"
==============================================================================

  B1 · The Rendering Equation                                     [L9A]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Why Blinn-Phong is wrong: not energy-conserving, not physically motivated
  Stage 2 · The rendering equation: L_o = L_e + ∫ f_r(ω_i,ω_o) L_i cosθ dω_i
  Stage 3 · Each term unpacked: outgoing radiance, emittance, BRDF, incident radiance
  Stage 4 · Why solving the integral is hard — infinite light bounces, the Monte Carlo problem
  Stage 5 · How real-time rendering approximates this (shadow maps, ambient terms, probes)

  B2 · PBR — Cook-Torrance BRDF                                   [L9B–L9C]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Microfacet theory: surfaces as statistical distribution of tiny mirrors
  Stage 2 · D term — GGX normal distribution: controls highlight shape vs roughness
  Stage 3 · G term — geometric self-shadowing (Smith method): masking and shadowing
  Stage 4 · F term — Fresnel-Schlick: reflectance increases at grazing angles
  Stage 5 · Full BRDF assembly: (D·G·F) / (4·cosθ_i·cosθ_o), energy conservation proof
  ── Project: PBR sphere scene, vary metallic + roughness, compare to Blinn-Phong ──

  B3 · HDR Framebuffer + Tone Mapping                             [L9D]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Why LDR fails with PBR: physical light values exceed 0–1 range
  Stage 2 · Floating-point FBO: GL_RGBA16F, rendering to HDR target
  Stage 3 · Reinhard tone mapping: compress HDR to LDR, preserve highlights
  Stage 4 · ACES filmic curve: industry standard, S-curve contrast
  Stage 5 · Gamma correction: linear workflow, sRGB output — why it matters
  ── Project addition: switch 03 scene to HDR pipeline ──

  B4 · Multiple Lights + Light Structures in GLSL                 [L9E]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Uniform arrays: uniform Light lights[N], struct in GLSL
  Stage 2 · Point light attenuation: 1/(k_c + k_l·d + k_q·d²) — derivation
  Stage 3 · Directional vs point vs spot: math differences, use cases
  Stage 4 · UBOs (Uniform Buffer Objects): share uniforms across shaders, binding points
  ── Project: add 4 coloured point lights to PBR scene ──

  THEME C — EFFICIENT COMPUTATION AT SCALE
  "How do you render this correctly without it being impossibly slow?"
==============================================================================

  C1 · Shadow Mapping                                             [L10A–L10B]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Core algorithm: render scene depth from light POV into shadow map texture
  Stage 2 · Sampling shadow map in fragment shader: compare fragment depth to stored depth
  Stage 3 · Shadow acne: cause (self-shadowing precision), fix (depth bias)
  Stage 4 · Peter-panning: over-bias side effect, how to tune
  Stage 5 · PCF (percentage closer filtering): sample kernel, soft shadow edges
  ── Project: add directional shadow to PBR scene ──

  C2 · Cascaded Shadow Maps (concept only)                        [L10C]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Why single shadow map fails at large distances: resolution problem
  Stage 2 · CSM: split frustum into depth ranges, separate shadow map per cascade
  Stage 3 · Cascade selection in shader, blend between cascades
  Stage 4 · Where this lives in production: Unreal's shadow system, Unity HDRP
  ── No implementation — concept and recognition only ──

  C3 · Deferred Rendering Architecture                            [L10D–L10E]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Forward rendering cost: O(fragments × lights) — why it breaks at 100+ lights
  Stage 2 · G-buffer design: albedo, world normal, depth, material params — layout decisions
  Stage 3 · Geometry pass: write G-buffer, no lighting computed here
  Stage 4 · Light pass: fullscreen quad, reconstruct world pos from depth, compute lighting
  Stage 5 · Tradeoffs: MSAA incompatibility, transparent object problem, memory bandwidth
  ── Project: port 03 scene to deferred renderer, add 10+ point lights ──

  C4 · Instanced Rendering + Frustum Culling                      [L11A–L11B]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Draw call overhead: why 10,000 individual draw calls kills performance
  Stage 2 · glDrawElementsInstanced + gl_InstanceID: per-instance transform via SSBO/VBO
  Stage 3 · Frustum culling: extract planes from projection matrix, AABB vs frustum test
  Stage 4 · Combining: cull CPU-side, submit only visible instances in one draw call
  ── Project: render 500 cubes instanced, profile vs naive loop in RenderDoc ──

  C5 · RenderDoc + GPU Profiling                                  [L11C]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Capture a frame: what RenderDoc shows (draw call list, state, textures)
  Stage 2 · Reading the G-buffer passes in your deferred renderer
  Stage 3 · Identifying bottlenecks: overdraw, texture fetch, vertex throughput
  Stage 4 · Timing individual passes: where is your frame budget going?
  ── Mandatory: profile your own deferred scene before moving to Theme D ──

  THEME D — SCREEN-SPACE APPROXIMATIONS
  "Approximate expensive global effects using only what's on screen"
==============================================================================

  D1 · Post-Processing Pipeline Architecture                      [L12A]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Fullscreen quad: render to FBO, then draw quad with result as texture
  Stage 2 · Ping-pong FBOs: chain multiple passes (blur → composite → output)
  Stage 3 · When to run post-process: after deferred light pass, before UI
  Stage 4 · Gamma + HDR in post-process chain: order matters

  D2 · SSAO (Screen-Space Ambient Occlusion)                      [L12B–L12C]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · What AO is: surfaces in crevices receive less ambient light
  Stage 2 · Screen-space limitation: only sees what's on screen, ignores occluders behind camera
  Stage 3 · Sample kernel: hemisphere of random samples in normal-oriented tangent space
  Stage 4 · Occlusion test: sample depth vs scene depth — count how many are occluded
  Stage 5 · Blur pass: noise reduction, bilateral blur to preserve edges
  ── Project addition: SSAO pass in deferred renderer ──

  D3 · Bloom                                                      [L12D]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Bright-pass extraction: threshold HDR buffer, keep pixels above cutoff
  Stage 2 · Gaussian blur: separable horizontal + vertical passes, kernel math
  Stage 3 · Additive composite: blend blurred bright buffer back onto scene
  Stage 4 · Tweaking: threshold, blur radius, blend strength — art vs engineering tradeoff

  D4 · Transparency + Blending                                    [L12E]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · GL_BLEND modes: src/dst factors, alpha blending equation
  Stage 2 · Order-dependence problem: transparent objects must sort back-to-front
  Stage 3 · OIT (Order Independent Transparency) concept: why sorting fails for intersecting geo
  Stage 4 · Practical approach: opaque deferred pass → forward transparent pass on top

  D5 · Anti-Aliasing                                              [L12F]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Why aliasing happens: sampling theorem, Nyquist rate on pixel grid
  Stage 2 · MSAA: supersample at rasterizer, average — why it breaks with deferred G-buffer
  Stage 3 · FXAA: post-process edge detection + blend — cheap, blurry
  Stage 4 · TAA: temporal accumulation across frames — industry standard since ~2016

  THEME E — GROUND-TRUTH LIGHT TRANSPORT
  "What is the correct answer that all real-time approximations are approximating?"
==============================================================================

  E1 · Ray Tracing Fundamentals                                   [L13A–L13B]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Ray definition: origin + direction, parametric form P(t) = O + t·D
  Stage 2 · Ray-sphere intersection: quadratic derivation, discriminant, t values
  Stage 3 · Ray-triangle intersection: Möller–Trumbore algorithm derivation
  Stage 4 · Ray-AABB intersection: slab method, tmin/tmax per axis, early exit
  Stage 5 · Shading at hit point: recover normal, apply Blinn-Phong or PBR at hit

  E2 · BVH — Bounding Volume Hierarchy                            [L13C]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Why naive: testing every ray against every triangle = O(rays × triangles)
  Stage 2 · BVH concept: recursively wrap geometry in AABBs, skip whole subtrees on miss
  Stage 3 · Midpoint split: simplest build strategy, why it fails for uneven distributions
  Stage 4 · SAH (Surface Area Heuristic): cost function, why it produces better trees
  Stage 5 · Traversal: stack-based, front-to-back order, early termination on hit

  E3 · Whitted Ray Tracer — Shadows, Reflections, Refraction      [L13D–L13E]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Shadow rays: cast toward light, any hit = shadowed — scene bias trick
  Stage 2 · Mirror reflection: reflect ray direction, recurse, clamp depth
  Stage 3 · Refraction: Snell's law derivation, total internal reflection condition
  Stage 4 · Recursive depth: why unbounded recursion explodes, depth limit
  Stage 5 · Image output: PPM format, gamma correction, tone mapping before write
  ── Project: Cornell box render — diffuse walls, mirror sphere, glass sphere ──

  E4 · Path Tracing Theory (no implementation)                    [L14A]
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stage 1 · Whitted limitation: only handles specular — misses diffuse inter-reflection
  Stage 2 · Monte Carlo integration: approximate the rendering equation integral via random sampling
  Stage 3 · Lambertian cosine-weighted hemisphere sampling: why cos-weighted not uniform
  Stage 4 · Convergence: why more samples = less noise, variance reduction strategies
  Stage 5 · Where this lives: Cycles (Blender), Arnold (film), Lumen (Unreal), OptiX/DXR

  F1 · Compute Shaders                                            [L15A–L15B]

  Stage 1 · What compute shaders are: GPGPU — arbitrary parallel computation
  Stage 2 · Workgroup size, dispatch dimensions: how threads are organized

  Stage 4 · Synchronization: memory barriers, why ordering matters on GPU

  F2 · GPU Particle System                                        [L15C]

  Stage 1 · Particle data layout in SSBO: position, velocity, lifetime, color
  Stage 2 · Compute pass: integrate velocity, apply gravity, kill dead particles
  Stage 3 · Render pass: read same SSBO in vertex shader, billboard quads
  Stage 4 · Sorting problem: transparent particles need depth sort (or OIT)
  ── Project: GPU particle system — fire or smoke effect ──

  F3 · SPH Fluid Simulation (concept + optional implementation)   [L15D–L15E]

  Stage 2 · Pressure + viscosity forces: Navier-Stokes in particle form
  Stage 3 · Spatial hashing: find neighbours without O(N²) search
  Stage 4 · GPU SPH: compute shader per-particle, grid cells in SSBO
  Stage 5 · Rendering fluid: metaballs, marching cubes, or screen-space fluid
  ── Portfolio differentiator — pursue if targeting simulation roles ──
